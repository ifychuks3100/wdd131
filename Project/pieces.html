Meet the Chess Pieces

Every chess piece has its own movement and purpose. Let's learn how they work.

♔ King

The king is the most important piece. It can move one square in any direction.

Value: Priceless

♕ Queen

The queen is the most powerful piece. It can move any number of squares horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

Value: 9 points

♖ Rook

The rook moves horizontally or vertically across any number of empty squares.

Value: 5 points

♗ Bishop

The bishop moves diagonally across any number of empty squares.

Value: 3 points

♘ Knight

The knight moves in an L-shape. It is the only piece that can jump over other pieces.

Value: 3 points

♙ Pawn

A pawn normally moves one square forward. On its first move, it can move two squares. It captures diagonally.

Value: 1 point
