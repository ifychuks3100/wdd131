Learn Basic Chess Strategies

Knowing how pieces move is only the beginning. Good strategies help you make better decisions.

1. Control the Centre

Try to control the four central squares of the chessboard. Controlling the centre gives your pieces more freedom to move and attack.

2. Develop Your Pieces

Bring your knights and bishops into the game early. Avoid leaving all your pieces on their starting squares.

3. Protect Your King

Your king must always be protected. Castling is one way to move your king to a safer position.

4. Avoid Losing Pieces

Before making a move, check whether your opponent can capture your piece.
